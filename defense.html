    What the numbers support

      Spotlight

      Tackles by game: stops vs. tackles after a successful play

      Success (for the opponent) means gaining 50% of the distance on 1st down, 70% on 2nd, and all of it on 3rd or 4th; TDs count, turnovers never do. Explosive = runs of 10+ and completions of 15+. Stuff = a run for zero or negative yards.

      Involvement = a scrimmage snap where the player is charted as the tackler or assist. Stop rate = share of those snaps where the offense fell short of success. Tackle depth = average yards the offense gained on those snaps (lower is better). Havoc = TFL, sack, INT, PBU or forced fumble.

      Adjusted stop rate = (stops + 10 × group rate) ÷ (involvements + 10). 80% range is a Wilson interval. Kickoff and punt coverage are left out. Position groups come from the roster: DL/DE = line, LB = linebackers, DB = secondary.

      The charts record who made the tackle, not who was on the field or who missed one, so this measures what happens when a player is involved rather than how often he's involved per snap. Snap counts would sharpen the "who could play more" read. Treat any flag as something to check on film.
